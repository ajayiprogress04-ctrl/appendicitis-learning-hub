# Objective-to-assessment map — 14 October 2026

The approved objective wording below is copied from the existing app without rewriting. The register identifies relevant evidence, not a blanket validity judgment.

## Objective 1

compare the accuracy of modified Alvarado score (MAS) alone versus MAS plus high sensitivity c-reactive protein (hsCRP) in the diagnosis of acute appendicitis

| Assessment | Status in reviewed source | Method/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Recorded study variables — Data and classification quality | assessed with limitations | Read-only completeness, dictionary-code, score-bound, negative-measurement, pathology-label and identical-record checks. |
| Histopathology — Reference-standard classification | assessed with limitations | Reconcile codes 1–5 positive and code 6 reactive lymphoid hyperplasia negative; examine label conflicts and missing/unexpected codes. |
| Fixed MAS and hs-CRP classifications — Independent diagnostic count reconciliation | assessed with limitations | Independently rebuild confusion counts at existing MAS ≥7 and CRP >3 rules on common eligible records and compare with stored fixed-strategy logs. |
| MAS, hs-CRP and AIR — Operational definitions and thresholds | pending evidence | Document MAS ≥7, hs-CRP >3 mg/L laboratory elevation, AIR ≥9 operational category and model held-out probability ≥0.50; verify against approved protocol. |
| Abdominal scan / proposed ultrasound — Index-test definition and indeterminacy | pending evidence | Verify modality, readers, blinding, timing and positive/negative/indeterminate code map. |
| WBC, neutrophils and other laboratory measures — Measurement and diagnostic criterion | pending evidence | Verify units, assay, timing and prespecified thresholds; continuous discrimination need not imply an operating cutoff. |
| Each eligible diagnostic combination — Rule fidelity and comparative performance | pending evidence | Register the actual rule; compare strategies on common eligible cases using paired tests and appropriate uncertainty. |
| Recruitment and specimen-linked cohort — Selection, spectrum and verification bias | pending evidence | Study flow, recruitment, excluded/non-operated cases, reference methods, timing and blinding review. |
| Exploratory Firth prediction models — Internal validation, calibration and overfitting | assessed with limitations | Audit within-fold standardisation and Firth fitting, held-out predictions, AUC, Brier score, log loss and calibration bins. |
| Prediction model transportability — External validation | pending evidence | Independent target-population data with frozen model and definitions; discrimination and calibration with uncertainty. |
| Every statistical output — Statistical method and reporting quality | assessable | Audit method, n, effect/interval/p fields, independence, pairing, expected counts, ordinal scales, missingness and multiplicity. |

## Actual findings and unresolved evidence

The accompanying JSON contains the complete source-data assessment register, 86 catalogue-output records and 14 supporting aggregate-template records. Six representative source outputs were calculated; unrun outputs remain unassessed. No participant records or identifiers are included.

## Not applicable

Cronbach alpha and factor analysis treating two single-item before/after confidence ratings as a multi-item scale are not applicable. Stable-condition test–retest reliability cannot be inferred from an information intervention. Prediction-model procedures are not automatically applied to hypothetical prespecified AND/OR rules. No automatic normality-test gate or p-value for descriptive rates is added.

## Evidence still needed

Approved thresholds and combination protocol; abdominal-scan modality and indeterminate coding; assay units/calibration and reference intervals; questionnaire item/anchor, expert/pilot and clinician linkage evidence; pathology procedures/blinding and recruitment flow; adequately supported subgroup comparisons; itemised costs, perspective, time horizon and diagnostic-pathway comparator; independent model-validation data.
